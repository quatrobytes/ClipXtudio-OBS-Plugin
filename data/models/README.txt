ClipXtudio local speech model
================================

File: ggml-tiny-q5_1.bin
Runtime: whisper.cpp (statically linked)
Languages: multilingual, including Spanish and English
Source:
https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp/resolve/main/ggml-tiny-q5_1.bin
SHA-256:
818710568DA3CA15689E31A743197B520007872FF9576237BDA97BD1B469C3D7

The model runs locally on audio frames supplied by the selected OBS source.
ClipXtudio does not upload local Voice Trigger audio or transcripts.
